# Salt All The Things 0.0.6

## Highlights

- Adds the authenticated persistence, API, and responsive Guest Bank experience for reusable guest records and many-to-many episode appearances.

## Fixes/Changes

- Adds private guest records, conflict-safe guest-to-show links, lifecycle controls, derived appearance statistics, and backward-compatible authenticated import/export fields.
- Adds `/guests.html` and authenticated navigation for creating, editing, searching, filtering, archiving, restoring, and safely deleting reusable guest records while reviewing escaped private notes and scheduled or unscheduled appearance history.
- Packages and serves the Guest Bank page and script from the same immutable application image as the API, with explicit static-asset deployment and non-production smoke coverage.
- Adds deterministic multi-guest controls to Show Management, allowing active guests to be reused across shows while displaying assigned and archived guests on planning cards, expanded preparation, and the full-screen authenticated show view.
- Refreshes canonical Guest Bank statistics after add/remove, scheduling, rescheduling, or idea deletion while keeping guest names and private notes out of public responses and Spotify/public overview text.
- Fixes the Show Management guest picker so clicks inside the Add guest disclosure reach the assignment handler without collapsing the show card.

## Validation

- Adds focused contract, API, persistence, lifecycle, concurrency, privacy, import/export, and reversible-migration tests for the Guest Bank foundation.
- Extends the frontend storage contract and isolated-environment deployment smoke checks for guest records and assignments.
- Keeps the complete pull-request suite on one asyncio loop so isolated PostgreSQL connections are closed deterministically during validation.
- Adds focused browser/static coverage for field validation, escaped markup, lifecycle feedback, protected deletion, searching and filtering, accurate scheduled/unscheduled statistics, navigation, responsive breakpoints, authentication hooks, and same-origin deployment boundaries.
- Adds browser/static and database coverage for deterministic guest picker state, multiple assignments, reuse across shows, archived historical display, private preparation rendering, idempotency, stale assignment conflicts, add/remove feedback, and public-overview exclusion.
- Adds a regression contract for the guest-picker event boundary that previously swallowed assignment clicks.
- Records owner validation in isolated development for guest creation and show assignment, including the corrected Add guest interaction, persistence after reload, and explicit slice approval.

## Deployment/Migrations

- Adds reversible Alembic revision `0009` for private guest and guest-assignment tables; development, test, and container validation require that single migration head.

## Rollback

- Application rollback remains compatible while revision `0009` is present; downgrading to `0008` removes guest records and links and therefore requires a verified environment-specific backup and separate data-recovery approval.

## Known Limitations

- Guest information remains authenticated planning data; no public guest credits, biography, contact, social, artwork, availability, outreach, or calendar features are included.
